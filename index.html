The People Who Inspire Me

James Francis Cameron

James Francis Cameron is a Canadian filmmaker and explorer. His films combine cutting-edge film technology with classical filmmaking techniques and have grossed over $10 billion worldwide, making him the second highest-grossing film director of all time. A major figure in the post-New Hollywood era, Cameron has received numerous accolades including three Academy Awards, two Primetime Emmy Awards, and four Golden Globe Awards, as well as nominations for six British Academy Film Awards.

Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist, scientist, philosopher of science, and world intellectual. Einstein is best known for his development of the theory of relativity, quantum mechanics, and scientific realism. For him, relativity and quantum mechanics are two pillars of modern physics.

René Descartes

René Descartes, also known as Renatus Cartesius in Latin literature, was a French philosopher and mathematician. His most important works are Discours de la méthode and Meditations de prima Philosophia. René Descartes is often called the father of modern philosophy
