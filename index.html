⭐The People Who Inspire Me🎨

⭐Merry Riana⭐

Merry Riana became a massive success and a universal source of inspiration because her journey represents a true "zero-to-hero" story born from extreme resilience, hard work, and a mission to give back. Forced to flee to Singapore due to the 1998 Jakarta riots, she survived on a tight student budget of instant noodles and cheap bread, only to defy the odds by earning her first $1 million at just 26 years old through grueling 14-hour workdays in financial services. People look up to her because her success wasn't handed to her; she faced countless rejections on the streets, paid off heavy student debts, and used her triumph to build the Merry Riana Group to empower others. Today, she continues to inspire millions across Indonesia as a content creator, educator, and public official, proving that financial limitations and difficult beginnings cannot stop anyone from achieving their ultimate dreams.

Maudy Ayunda

Maudy Ayunda is a powerful inspiration for women because she breaks old stereotypes by balancing a highly successful entertainment career with world-class education at Oxford and Stanford. Instead of relying on fame alone, she prioritizes learning, runs her own skincare business, and manages a foundation that provides scholarships and leadership mentorship to youth. By maintaining a positive, scandal-free reputation and openly sharing practical advice on personal growth, she empowers women to independent, ambitious, and confident in chasing their own multi-passionate dreams.

🎸Park Eun Bin💎

Park Eun-bin is a top South Korean actress born on September 4, 1992. She started her career as a child model at just 4 years old. She won the prestigious Grand Prize (Daesang) at the Baeksang Arts Awards for her iconic role in Extraordinary Attorney Woo. Many people admire her because she is a true all-rounder artist who excels at everything she does. She is an extraordinary actress who transforms into every character completely. She is also a skilled musician who plays the violin and piano, and she recently debuted as a solo singer with a beautiful vocal range. In her free time, she is even talented at painting and drawing.
